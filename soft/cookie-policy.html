How cookies work here

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Nexus Digital uses them to help the website function, remember preferences, and understand broad usage patterns.

Essential cookies

These cookies support core features such as security, form handling, and session behavior. The website may not work correctly without them.

Optional analytics

Where analytics are enabled, they help us understand which pages are useful and where the experience can improve. These tools are configured to collect only aggregated or appropriately protected information.

Managing cookies

You can review or remove cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect site functionality. For questions, email hello@nexusdigital.com.
